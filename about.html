About Us

Built for Better Sleep

SleepCycle Pro was created with one mission: use science to help people wake up refreshed, sharp, and ready — every single day. Completely free, forever.

Our Mission

At SleepCycle Pro, we believe that quality sleep is not a luxury — it is a fundamental human right and the single most powerful tool available for physical health, mental performance, emotional wellbeing, and longevity. Yet most people are unknowingly sabotaging their sleep quality with something as simple as a poorly timed alarm.

Our mission is to make sleep science accessible to everyone. The 90-minute sleep cycle model has been understood by researchers since 1953, yet most people still set their alarms for arbitrary round numbers — 6:00 AM, 7:00 AM, 8:00 AM — with no regard for where that time lands in their natural sleep architecture. The result is the epidemic of morning grogginess, cognitive fog, and chronic fatigue that affects hundreds of millions of people daily.

SleepCycle Pro exists to solve this problem with a simple, free, science-backed calculator that takes the research out of the lab and puts it in your hands. No app downloads. No subscriptions. No personal data collection. Just clear, accurate, personalized sleep timing guidance available to anyone with a browser.

The Science Behind Our Tool

SleepCycle Pro is built on decades of validated sleep science research. Here are the core scientific foundations of our calculator:

🧬
90-Minute Ultradian Rhythm

Discovered by Kleitman & Aserinsky (1953), validated through 70+ years of polysomnography research across thousands of subjects worldwide.

📋
AASM 2025 Sleep Duration Guidelines

Age-specific recommendations from the American Academy of Sleep Medicine's 2025 consensus — meta-analysis of 864 peer-reviewed studies.

⏱️
Sleep Latency Research

Default 14-minute latency based on Ohayon et al. meta-analysis (n=10,170), the most comprehensive normative sleep latency dataset available.

🎭
REM Distribution Model

REM duration estimates based on the well-established progressive front-to-back loading pattern documented across thousands of polysomnography studies.

😵
Sleep Inertia Research

Sleep inertia (the grogginess from waking mid-cycle) documented by Walter Reed Army Institute (2023) — up to 57% reaction time reduction.
