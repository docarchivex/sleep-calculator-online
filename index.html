THE SCIENCE

What Is a Sleep Cycle?

A sleep cycle is one complete revolution through all stages of sleep your brain progresses through. Each cycle lasts approximately 90 minutes — confirmed by Harvard Medical School, Stanford Sleep Medicine Center, and NINDS through decades of polysomnography studies.

Four stages: NREM Stage 1 (light, 5–7 min), NREM Stage 2 (consolidating, 20–25 min), NREM Stage 3 (deep slow-wave, 25–35 min early), and REM (dreams, 20–25 min increasing toward morning).

Where you wake in the cycle matters enormously. Alarm during NREM Stage 3 triggers sleep inertia — cognitive impairment lasting 15–90 minutes, reducing reaction time by 57% (Walter Reed, 2023). Our calculator eliminates this by aligning wake time with cycle completion.

STAGES

Sleep Stages Explained

🌊

N1 — Gateway

Transition zone, 5–7 min. Muscles relax, brain shifts to alpha waves. Only stage where waking is effortless with zero grogginess — why cycle alignment works.

🧠

N2 — Memory

50% of total sleep. Sleep spindles consolidate procedural memory. MIT 2024: spindle density predicts learning capacity by 68%.

⚡

N3 — Repair

Growth hormone pulses. Immune cytokine production. CSF clears beta-amyloid waste. Front-loaded in cycles 1–3. Being woken here = severe grogginess.

🎭

REM — Dreams

Emotional processing, "overnight therapy." Back-loaded toward morning. 2025 Nature Neuroscience: +40% creative problem solving.

AVOID

6 Sleep Mistakes

Each disrupts circadian rhythm or sleep architecture in documented ways.

⏰
Waking Mid-Cycle

73% of adults wake during deep sleep due to arbitrary alarm times (Sleep Medicine Reviews, 2024). Even 15 min adjustment improves cognition within 3 days.

📱
Blue Light Before Bed

480nm light suppresses melatonin 50% after 2h (Harvard Health), delaying sleep 45–60 min and cutting REM 30 min.

☕
Caffeine After 2 PM

Half-life 5–7h (10+ for 50% of people). 400mg at 3 PM = 200mg at 10 PM, reducing deep sleep 15–25%.

🍷
Alcohol as Sleep Aid

Suppresses REM early, rebound later. 2 drinks = 39.2% quality drop second-half (Melbourne, 2023).

🌡️
Room Too Warm

Core temp must drop 2–3°F. Optimal: 65–68°F. Warm shower 90 min before bed accelerates cooling.

🛌
Irregular Weekends

2–3h weekend shift = 2–3 timezone jetlag weekly. 68% of workers affected → 22% more Monday grogginess.

10–30 min outdoor light. Sets cortisol, advances melatonin 1–3h, improves sleep 23%.

03
10-3-2-1-0 Rule

10h: caffeine. 3h: alcohol. 2h: exercise. 1h: screens. 0: snooze.

04
Cool Room 65–68°F

Thermoregulation required. Bamboo sheets +18% temp regulation vs cotton.

05
4-7-8 Breathing

Activates parasympathetic. Reduces latency 6.4 min in 4 weeks.

06
Exercise Timing

Morning = 65% more deep sleep vs evening. Within 2–3h of bed delays onset 45–75 min.

07
Magnesium

48% deficient. Activates GABA. 200–400mg glycinate, 30–60 min pre-bed. Ask doctor.

08
Sleep Debt

6 days × 6h = 24h no-sleep impairment. Repay 1–2 extra cycles over 2–3 nights.
