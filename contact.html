How to Write an Effective Email

Help us help you faster by including these details in your email:

1Use a clear subject line that describes your issue — e.g., "Bug: Wake Up Mode calculation wrong" or "Feature Request: Dark mode option."
2For bug reports, describe exactly what you did, what you expected to happen, and what actually happened. Include your browser name and version.
3For calculation questions, include the mode you used (Sleep Now / Bedtime / Wake Up), the time you entered, your age group, and sleep latency setting.
4Screenshots are incredibly helpful for UI issues. Most devices support screenshots natively — attach them to your email.
5For privacy requests (GDPR/CCPA), include "Privacy Request" in the subject and specify which right you are exercising (access, deletion, etc.).
